# Universal AI Cartoon Studio — Master Requirements v1.0

## Mission
Build a production-ready, locally operated, AI-assisted 3D animation studio for multiple original cartoon series. The Creative Director remains the final approval authority.

## Required capability domains
1. Scene and spatial intelligence.
2. Character identity, proportions, rigs, clothing and references.
3. Body/facial acting, cycles, slapstick timing and dialogue synchronization.
4. Camera, lighting, blocking, continuity, Sequencer and rendering.
5. Dependencies, jobs, versions, approvals, history and templates.
6. Independent deterministic and visual quality assurance.

## Mandatory safety
- Inspect before selecting or installing.
- Verify source, documentation, license, compatibility and maintenance.
- Keep protected originals separate and immutable.
- Use isolated environments and loopback-only services.
- Record every component and production action.
- Back up before configuration changes.
- Do not equate script completion with artistic correctness.
- Stop for approval after every major phase.

## Operational success gate
The system is operational only after verified demonstrations of read-only Blender inspection, spatial-error detection, isolated asset creation and registration, Unreal transfer, controlled cinematic render, complete job evidence and rollback after failure.
